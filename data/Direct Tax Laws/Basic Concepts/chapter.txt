Basic Concepts of Direct Tax Laws

1. Overview of Income Tax Law in India
The Constitution of India (Article 265) states that no tax shall be levied or collected except by authority of law. The power to levy direct taxes is given to the Central Government under Entry 82 of the Union List (Seventh Schedule to Article 246).

2. Components of Income Tax Law
- Income-tax Act, 2025: Governs the levy of income tax. Came into force on 1st April 2026.
- Annual Finance Act: Passed every year, specifies the rates of tax.
- Income-tax Rules, 2026: Framed by CBDT.
- Circulars/Notifications: Issued by CBDT/Central Government. Circulars bind the department, not the assessee.
- Legal Decisions of Courts.

3. Charge of Income Tax (Section 4)
Income-tax is charged at the rates specified for the tax year by the Finance Act or the Income-tax Act on the total income of every person.

4. Important Definitions
- Assessee: A person by whom any tax or any other sum is payable under the Act.
- Person: Includes Individual, HUF, Company, Firm (including LLP), AOP, BOI, Local Authority, and Artificial Juridical Person.

5. Capital vs Revenue Receipts
- Income-tax is generally levied on revenue receipts, not capital receipts, unless specifically provided (e.g., capital gains).
- Compensation on termination of agency is a capital receipt but taxable under section 26(2)(b).

6. Application vs Diversion of Income
- Application of income: Using income to discharge a self-imposed obligation (taxable).
- Diversion of income by overriding title: Income diverted at source before reaching the assessee under a legal/contractual obligation (not taxable).

7. Rates of Tax, Surcharge & Cess
- Default tax regime (Section 202): 
  - Upto ₹4,00,000: NIL
  - ₹4,00,001 to ₹8,00,000: 5%
  - ₹8,00,001 to ₹12,00,000: 10%
  - ₹12,00,001 to ₹16,00,000: 15%
  - ₹16,00,001 to ₹20,00,000: 20%
  - ₹20,00,001 to ₹24,00,000: 25%
  - Above ₹24,00,000: 30%

- Optional tax regime: 
  - Basic exemption: ₹2,50,000 for normal individuals.
  - Senior citizens (60-79 years): ₹3,00,000.
  - Very senior citizens (80+ years): ₹5,00,000.

- Surcharge for Individuals/HUF/AOP/BOI:
  - 10% for income > ₹50 lakhs up to ₹1 crore.
  - 15% for income > ₹1 crore up to ₹2 crores.
  - 25% for income > ₹2 crores up to ₹5 crores.
  - 37% for income > ₹5 crores.
  - (Note: Surcharge on dividends and capital gains u/s 196, 197, 198 is capped at 15%).

- Surcharge for Domestic Companies:
  - 7% for income > ₹1 crore up to ₹10 crores.
  - 12% for income > ₹10 crores.

- Marginal Relief:
  Marginal Relief = Total tax + surcharge on total income - (Tax on threshold limit + amount of income exceeding threshold).
  Ensures the increase in tax doesn't exceed the increase in income beyond the threshold (e.g. ₹50L, ₹1Cr, ₹2Cr, ₹10Cr).

- Health and Education Cess: 4% on income tax + surcharge.

- Rebate u/s 156: 
  - Under Default Regime: Total income up to ₹12,00,000 gets rebate up to ₹60,000. Marginal rebate applies if income slightly exceeds ₹12,00,000.
  - Under Optional Regime: Total income up to ₹5,00,000 gets rebate up to ₹12,500.
