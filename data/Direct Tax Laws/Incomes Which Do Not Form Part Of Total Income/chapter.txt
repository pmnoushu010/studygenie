Incomes Which Do Not Form Part of Total Income

1. Agricultural Income [Schedule II, Table: Sl. No. 1]
Agricultural income is entirely exempt from central income tax. It includes:
- Rent or revenue from agricultural land situated in India.
- Income from agricultural operations (tilling, sowing, etc.) and subsequent operations.
- Income from farm buildings in the immediate vicinity of agricultural land.
- Income from saplings or seedlings grown in a nursery.

Not treated as agricultural income:
- Income from breeding of livestock, poultry farming, fisheries, dairy farming.
- Sale of trees of spontaneous growth.
- Rent from letting out houses on agricultural land for residential purposes of industrial workers.

Urban vs Rural Agricultural Land:
Land is urban if located within municipality/cantonment limits with a population ≥ 10,000. Or, if outside, based on aerial distance:
- > 10,000 upto 1,00,000: within 2 km.
- > 1,00,000 upto 10,00,000: within 6 km.
- > 10,00,000: within 8 km.
Capital gains on transfer of urban agricultural land is taxable; rural agricultural land is exempt.

2. Apportionment of Income (Rules 270 and 271)
When agricultural produce is used as raw material in a business, the market value of the agricultural produce is allowed as a deduction from business income and treated as agricultural income.

Prescribed Apportionment Percentages (Rule 271):
- Sale of rubber products manufactured in India: 65% Agricultural, 35% Business.
- Sale of coffee grown and cured in India: 75% Agricultural, 25% Business.
- Sale of coffee grown, cured, roasted, and grounded in India: 60% Agricultural, 40% Business.
- Sale of tea grown and manufactured in India: 60% Agricultural, 40% Business.

3. Partial Integration of Agricultural Income
Applicability: Individuals, HUFs, AOPs, BOIs, Artificial Juridical Persons. (Not applicable to companies, firms, LLPs).
Conditions: 
1) Net agricultural income > ₹5,000 p.a.
2) Non-agricultural income > basic exemption limit.

Computation Steps:
Step 1: Compute tax on (Non-agricultural income + Net agricultural income) -> Xn
Step 2: Compute tax on (Net agricultural income + Basic Exemption Limit) -> Yn
Step 3: Tax chargeable = Xn - Yn. (Then add surcharge and cess).

4. Other Exempt Incomes
- Share of profit from a partnership firm: Exempt in the hands of the partner (already taxed at firm level).
- HUF receipts by members: Exempt.
- Compensation on account of disaster: Exempt if granted by Government.
- Payments to MPs/MLAs: Daily allowance and constituency allowance are exempt.

5. Restrictions on Allowability of Expenditure (Section 14)
- No deduction is allowed for expenditure incurred in relation to income that does not form part of total income.
- Applicable even if the exempt income has not accrued or been received in that year.
- If the Assessing Officer is not satisfied, they can determine disallowance as: Direct Expenditure + Indirect Expenditure (1% of the annual average of monthly averages of investment balances).
